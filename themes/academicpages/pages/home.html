I am a Ph.D. candidate in Earth Science at Cornell University, advised by Professor Chloé Arson, with minors in Computer Science and Scientific Computing. Before Cornell, I earned my B.S. in Mining Engineering at Northeastern University in 2024, where I worked on deep learning for microseismic signal analysis and tunnel boring machines (TBMs).

My research connects scientific AI with computational geomechanics, with applications in subsurface energy systems.

I work on differentiable programming, neural PDE solvers, uncertainty-aware scientific AI, agentic AI, and AI for scientific discovery. These methods complement my work in homogenization and coupled multiphysics simulation to study geothermal reservoirs, hydraulic fracturing, and the interactions between fluid flow, heat transport, and rock deformation.
